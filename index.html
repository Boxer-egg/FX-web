02 — Features

Thoughtfully
designed.

01

Two Layer Modes

Place particles above all windows for maximum drama, or slip them quietly below — floating on your wallpaper while you work.

02

Menubar Native

No Dock icon. No floating window. Lives silently in your menu bar — ⌘⇧T to toggle on and off in an instant.

03

Real-time Sliders

Tune density, speed, size, opacity, and wind directly from the menu. Changes apply live, no settings window required.

04

Wind Simulation

Dial in horizontal acceleration for each effect. A gentle breeze or a full storm — the physics respond to your input.

05

SpriteKit Rendering

Built on Apple's native SpriteKit for hardware-accelerated particle rendering. Smooth at any density, minimal CPU impact.

06

5 Languages

Full localization in English, Chinese, Japanese, German, and French. The interface speaks your language.
